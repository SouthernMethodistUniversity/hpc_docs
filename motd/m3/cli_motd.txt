POLICIES:
---------

Users must comply with all applicable laws, regulations, and SMU policies when
using HPC resources. This includes, but is not limited to, SMU’s acceptable use
policy: https://www.smu.edu/policy/8-information-technology/8-1-acceptable-use

Any commands, job submissions, file operations, processes, network access, etc. 
that jeopardize the security or stability of the HPC systems or negatively
impacts the ability of other researchers to use the systems may be terminated
without warning.

AI Usage:
---------

Users are expected to maintain meaningful human oversight of all AI tools and
agents. Providing an AI tool or agent autonomous or unattended control of HPC
system commands, job submission, file operations, processes, network access, 
etc. is prohibited. Usage of AI tools and agents is only allowed if you 
explicitly review and approve the commands, code, or other actions suggested by
the AI tools or agents.

=====================
$WORK is End of Life!
=====================

As of August 15, 2025 $WORK is no longer supported. 
Any data remaining in $WORK should be moved as soon as possible.
$WORK directories are subject to be deprovisioned (permanently deleted).

Please contact help@smu.edu with [HPC] in the subject line with any questions
or concerns.

========================
Automatic $SCRATCH Purge
========================

Beginning on July 1, 2026, the automations will be enabled to enforce
the 60-day purge policy for $SCRATCH.  Files older than 60 days stored
in $SCRATCH will be automatically and permanently deleted.
